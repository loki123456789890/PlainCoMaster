# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Primary: budget-conscious shoppers in the Philippines browsing PlainCo on mobile, in short casual sessions, price-and-deal driven, mostly on mobile data. Checkout habits lean COD-first, so trust in the transaction matters as much as trust in the product. Secondary: store admins and support staff who work the AdminDashboard, Products, Orders, Users, and Support screens as an internal operations tool.

## Product Purpose

PlainCo is a mobile-first marketplace for secondhand (ukay-ukay) and ready-to-wear clothing in the Philippines. It exists to make buying used clothing feel as safe and easy as buying new, in one catalog rather than two separate shopping experiences. Success looks like repeat purchases and shoppers trusting the condition and quality of what they buy sight-unseen.

## Positioning

One catalog, both worlds: ukay-ukay and ready-to-wear sit side by side in the same browsing experience, distinguished by type tags rather than split into separate apps, tabs, or sections.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, unpretentious. Plain-spoken over promotional — the voice reads like a helpful local shop, not a flash-sale marketplace.

## Anti-references

Not Shopee/Lazada-style marketplace clutter: no countdown timers, no stacked discount badges, no red/orange flash-sale banners fighting for attention. Also not sterile minimalist tech — the app should feel approachable and human, not clinical or corporate.

## Design Principles

- **One catalog, both worlds.** Ukay-ukay and ready-to-wear never fork into visually separate experiences; a badge or tag distinguishes type, the layout and flow stay the same.
- **Trust reads through consistency, not badges.** The same Button/Input/Card/Badge vocabulary everywhere is what makes buying secondhand feel safe — inconsistency reads as risk.
- **Warm color, not warm chaos.** Clay, moss, and gold are used deliberately and restrained; they signal warmth through accent and typography, never through decorative clutter or default-neutral scaffolding.
- **Plain-spoken over promotional.** Copy and layout stay unpretentious — skip countdown timers, aggressive discount badges, and flash-sale grammar even where a marketplace competitor would reach for them.
- **One system, aware of both OSes.** PlainCo's own component vocabulary (Button, Input, Card, Badge, EmptyState) is the source of truth on iOS and Android; native conventions (gestures, safe areas, navigation patterns) are respected without overriding the brand system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard mobile accessibility baseline: solid color contrast, readable type sizes, VoiceOver/TalkBack support, and respect for reduced-motion preferences. No formal WCAG target beyond good practice.
